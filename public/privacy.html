Who controls this data

BBTSL is the controller for personal data processed through bbtsl.lol. You can contact the project team at bbtsl@pve.bio. BBTSL does not currently appoint a data protection officer.

This notice is written to provide the information required by GDPR Articles 12 to 14. The European Commission explains the required information and your rights in its Article 13 guidance and the binding text is available in GDPR Article 13.

Optional analytics and your choice

Google Analytics is optional and is not configured in every deployment. When it is configured, the Google script is not loaded and no analytics request is made while your choice is unknown or denied. Selecting “Accept analytics” stores the choice in your browser and loads the analytics script. Selecting “Reject analytics” stores a denial and does not load it.

The “Cookie settings” control remains available so you can change your decision. Revoking consent sets analytics storage to denied, removes BBTSL-accessible Google Analytics cookies, and reloads the page without the analytics script. Clearing site storage also clears the saved choice.

Discord sign-in and the private integration API

Discord sign-in is optional. When you select it, Discord authenticates you and returns the identity fields listed above. Discord processes its own services under its Privacy Policy.

The private, read-only BBTSL integration API uses rotating credentials derived from separately managed client secrets. The website does not expose those client secrets to the browser. This policy covers the website and private integration API only.

Sharing and international transfers

We use Cloudflare to host, secure, and deliver the website and its database. Cloudflare acts as a processor for customer content and request data where applicable. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for its processing information.

Website fonts are served from BBTSL. Opening a page does not request fonts from a third-party font provider.

Discord receives information needed for its OAuth sign-in flow. Google receives analytics information only after consent and only when analytics is configured. Cloudflare, Google, and Discord may process data outside your country, including outside the EEA or UK. Their own notices describe the safeguards and transfer arrangements they use. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

  • Session cookies last up to 12 hours. The short OAuth state cookie lasts up to 10 minutes.
  • Rate-limit and security admission records are short-lived and are pruned automatically.
  • Discord profile and role records remain while the account is needed for access control or public team attribution. Disabled accounts are not shown publicly.
  • Revertible staff audit snapshots and detached media remain available for no more than 14 days from the original audit action. At the exact deadline, the private media copy and reversible snapshots are disposed of.
  • The remaining visual audit record, including actor, action, time, summary, and non-reversible change information, remains for no more than 90 days from the original action and is then deleted.
  • Public item and active media records remain until changed or removed by authorised staff. Detached media that is not linked to a reversible audit action receives a bounded cleanup deadline.
  • Your analytics-consent choice remains in browser local storage until you change it or clear site storage. Google controls the retention configured for the associated Analytics property.

If you request erasure, we will assess the request against our security, integrity, and legal obligations.

Your GDPR rights

Subject to the conditions in applicable law, you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, withdraw consent, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live, work, or believe an infringement occurred. The European Commission lists these rights in its GDPR rights guidance.

Email bbtsl@pve.bio with “Privacy Request” in the subject line. Tell us which Discord account or email address to use to locate the record. We may ask for proportionate proof of identity before disclosing or changing account data. We aim to respond within one month, as described in GDPR Article 12.

Changes

We will update this policy when the website, security controls, private integration API, analytics configuration, or legal requirements materially change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
